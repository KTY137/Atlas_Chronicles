# Die Schwelle — Product Candidate B, Round 5

> **Die Tür funktioniert nur, wenn sie selten ist.**
> — [`fixtures/eron/WELT.md`](../../fixtures/eron/WELT.md) §5.2, about Kaya's own wiki, written before
> this candidate existed.

**The thesis, one line:** **Ein roter Link ist keine Tür. Eine Schwelle ist eine Tür — und es gibt
elf davon, nicht 1 253.** Most absences in a real world are not invitations; they are *finished
naming*. This candidate makes the distinction between a **door** (something the table can move toward
and resolve with a die) and an **absence** (a name that is doing its whole job by existing)
load-bearing in the data model, in the renderer, in the canvas and in the economy.

**It optimises for** the GM whose wiki is the measured mess — 69.3 % red, 9.3 doors per article, four
spellings of one country, the most-wanted missing page a *language* — which is 100 % of real GMs,
including the stakeholder. It optimises for **the moment of choosing**, not the moment of supply. And
it optimises for a product that can be trusted about its own numbers, because the number it has been
publishing is seven times too large.

**At the cost of, stated as costs:**

- **The headline number shrinks by ~97 %**, in public, on purpose. `1 253 Türen` becomes `elf
  Schwellen` and, at the table, `drei`. The lineage's most-marketed sentence gets smaller before it
  gets truer.
- **The GM who just wants to write an article is served worse than by the benchmark.** World Anvil's
  *Inline Article Creation* (released **2026-01-14**, free and Guild tiers alike —
  [`04-die-eine-plattform.md`](../../04-die-eine-plattform.md) §0.2) turns a red link into a text
  editor in one keypress. This candidate deliberately does not, for 678 of 689 names. §7 concedes
  it plainly.
- **A classifier now stands between the author and her own world**, and when it is wrong it is wrong
  *with a printed reason*, which is worse than being wrong quietly. §11.1.
- **The canvas enters slice 1** after four rounds of deferral, and its day count is a guess in an
  uncalibrated unit ([`RB-18`](../../research/RB-18-widerspruch.md)). §10, §11.5.
- **And the one that could sink it:** shrinking the menu is not the same as increasing the appetite.
  If gate W1 returns the champion's own number, this candidate bought nothing and paid for a
  classifier. §11.2 is the honest version and it is the hard one.

**What it is not.** It is not a repair of Die Woche. Die Woche is carried whole — die Vollmacht, der
Brief, der Umbruch, die Wiederkehr, Nachrechnen, die Fokuswache, the five mint handlers, `Sicht`, B9,
der Zwillingsbeweis, die Herkunftsschicht. **One variant of one column is deleted** (§9), and
everything else in [`CHAMPION.md`](../CHAMPION.md) stands unless this document changes it.

**The fork with the rival, stated once so the round is not wasted.** Der Schlüsselmeister reads
RB-12 §5.4's *113 doors against ~5 keys per player-week* as a **supply crisis** and mints more keys.
This candidate reads the same line as a **demand fiction** and deletes the doors. **Both cannot be
right, and one measurement separates us:** hand-label 100 redlinks from the real corpus for
*"could a party ride at this, this month?"*. If the labelled door-rate is high, the rival is right and
the constraint really is keys. If it is low — and RB-21a's 14.5 % and RB-12's 68.5 %-linked-once say
it will be — then minting more keys buys the GM a longer menu of things she was never going to cook.

## 2. The flex

**„Dein Wiki hat vier Löcher. Wir haben ein Land."**

It is a Sunday afternoon. Kaya has never opened this product before. Timo is on the sofa. She drags
`eron-export.xml` — the real 351 KB MediaWiki export, 74 pages — onto the window.

**Beat one — forty seconds, and then a balance sheet, not a progress bar.**

```
Übernahme abgeschlossen · 41 s

  74 Artikel        1 075 Passagen        1 253 rote Kanten
                                          ─────────────────
                                          689 Namen
                                          ── davon 11 Schwellen
```

**Beat two.** Timo: *„Und die anderen 678?"* — She presses **Die Namen**. No list of broken links,
no repair queue, no red. A single column of names set in the article's own type, alphabetical,
`Andarisch · Baron · Blattheim · Chi-Sen · Erzherzog · Gluthafen · Herzog · Jarl …`, and one line
beneath it:

> **678 Namen. Fertig.**

**Beat three — the product refuses, and prints why.** She hovers `Andarisch`. It does not light up.
It has no cursor change and no click target. The gutter prints one line:

```
Andarisch · Sprache · in 18 Artikeln genannt · 41 Belegstellen
keine Schwelle: nur in Feldern »Sprache« genannt — man kann nicht hingehen.        [ doch → ]
```

**Beat four — the „wait, what?"** She opens **Die Schwellen**. Eleven rows. Row three:

```
Geeintes Elfenreich Demmaros                       Reich · 9 Artikel · 12 Nennungen
   auch geschrieben:  Geeinte Elfenreich Demmaros ·  Geeinigtes Elfenreich Demmaros
                      Vereinigtes Elfenreich Demmaros
   Grund: Ortsfeld »sitz=« in 2 Artikeln · Marker auf Karte:Andaria
   ▸ vier rote Links im Wiki.  Eine Schwelle.
```

> **Timo:** *„Warte. Dein Wiki hat da vier Löcher reingeschrieben und ihr habt daraus ein Land
> gemacht?"*

**Beat five — and this is the beat the wiki half alone cannot reach.** Saturday, 21:47, session
fifteen. The party rides north. Kaya opens **der Tisch**. On the canvas — a real grid, real tokens,
per-character fog — there is a **thin unclosed ring** at `[1185, 5518]` on Andaria's coordinate
plane, with a label and a distance:

```
◌  Blattheim          unbeschrieben · 3 Tage Ritt · 14 Artikel nennen es
```

Sera's player holds four passages naming Blattheim, so she sees the ring. Brannt's player holds none,
so **for him the tile is empty fog and the DOM is byte-identical to a reader with no campaign at
all** (der Zwillingsbeweis, CHAMPION §6, now run over the canvas). They ride. The ring resolves with
a roll, and a paragraph lands in the encyclopedia with a Saturday over it.

> **A red link with a position is not a link. It is a place you are three days' ride from.**

**Why this is the right flex, and why it only exists under this thesis.** A threshold can afford to
carry a spatial identity — an `Ort` row, an `Anker`, a distance, a fog projection, a ring on a canvas
— *only because there are eleven of them*. You cannot give 1 253 red links a body: RB-21a measured
that only 182 of them point at anything spatial at all, and RB-12 measured a single passage carrying
**77** of them. **Scarcity is what buys the address.** The champion's door was a text decoration in an
infobox row; this one is an object in the world with coordinates, and the reason it can be is that
this candidate threw 678 of its siblings away.

**And it photographs in one frame** — the four spellings collapsing into one country is a single
screenshot, and it is the one screenshot in this lineage that makes a stranger say *"my wiki does
that too"* before it makes them say *"wait, what?"*.

## 3. Das Level-Up — die Vortragsfeatures

Four capabilities CHAMPION v5 does not have. All four fall out of one sentence — *most absences are
not doors* — and three of them are the same table read three ways.

### 3.1 VF1 · Die Namensprüfung und der Grund — the machine that turns 1 253 into 3

**What it is.** A four-stage, deterministic, **no-AI, no-heuristic-on-word-length** classifier that
runs at import and continuously afterwards, and that prints its reason for every verdict.

```
1 253 rote Kanten
  −  28  Notation                       n. K. · n. K · v. K. · v. K   [gemessen, RB-12 §5.3]
= 1 225 Kanten auf 685 Ziele
  ⇣  Referent-Kollaps                   ~22 Ziele → 9 Referenten      [verifiziert, WELT §3.3(b)]
=  672 Referenten
  ⇣  Nachfrage ≥ 3 Artikel (nach Kollaps)                             [gemessen, RB-12 §5.4]
=  ~113 Kandidaten                      — 83 % fallen in EINEM Schritt weg
  ⇣  Erreichbarkeit                     Ortsfeld | Personenfeld | Kartenmarker
=  DIE SCHWELLEN
  ⇣  Nähe (im Spiel, pro Szene)         Raum-Distanz vom Standort der Gruppe
=  ~3 am Tisch
```

**The stage that is new, and it is the whole idea: Erreichbarkeit is read off the label the author
already typed.** Not from the word. Not from a model. From *where the link sits*:

| Reachable — a `Schwelle` may be minted | Not reachable — stays a `Nennung` |
|---|---|
| Infobox labels that are **spatial**: `Heimat`, `sitz`, `regierungssitz`, `Geburtsort`, `Todesort`, `Hauptstadt` | `Sprache`, `Kaste`, `Klasse`, `Effekt`, `Währung`, `Epoche` |
| Infobox labels that name a **person**: `Verwandte`, `anführer`, `Gemahlin`, `Vater` | Labels that name an **attribute** of the subject |
| A title that is a **marker on a `Karte`** (187 of Eron's 190 markers have no article) | A title appearing only inside body prose, never in a field |
| Explicit GM promotion (**die Beförderung**, §3.4) | Everything else |

**Why that is not a hack.** It is the same mechanism WELT.md §3.4 identifies as the strongest
"better than Fandom" argument in the whole corpus: *„Der Typ muss aus der Struktur folgen, die der
Autor ohnehin ausfüllt — nicht aus einer zweiten Pflicht, die er ignoriert."* Categories were used by
**3 of 74** articles and two of those three were emitted automatically by a template. Infobox labels
were used by **~590 field rows with exactly 6 empty values**. The authors already told us which of
their links point at places. Nobody has ever asked them.

**The Arvex test, and it is the demo sentence.** `Arvex Aurelius Paradon` — the last emperor, 18
infobox rows, **zero prose** (WELT.md §3.2; RB-13 target **M18**). Under this classifier:

```
Sprache   Andarisch                                   → Nennung   (Feld »Sprache«)
Heimat    Gluthafen, Königreich Eromir (Bis 751) …    → Schwellen-Kandidaten (Feld »Heimat«)
Titel     Kaiser, König von Terabur, Herzog von …     → Nennungen (Amt ist kein Ort)
Kaste     Hochadel → Adel in Andaria                  → Nennung
```

**Two rows of the same infobox on the same article, one a name and one a door, and the difference is
a word the author typed in 2023 without thinking about it.**

**What the user does.** Nothing, on import. Afterwards: reads the reason on any name, and presses
`doch →` when the machine is wrong. One keypress, audited, permanent, and it teaches the rule (§3.4).

**Why only under this thesis.** A product that treats every red link as a door has nothing to
classify. The classifier is not an optimisation of the champion's door — it is the object the
champion's door was missing, and it only becomes worth building once you accept that the answer is
*mostly no*.

**Cost.** ~6 days: normaliser + alias clustering (2), the label→reachability table as a declarative
package (1), the reason-string layer and the reversal audit (2), the ranking (1). **Risk: high** —
not in the code, in the *precision*. §12 states the gate.

### 3.2 VF2 · Die Schwelle hat einen Körper — the door becomes an object on the map

**What it is.** Every `Schwelle` carries a spatial identity: an `Ort` row in RB-21a's strict `Raum`
tree, and — where the corpus supplies one — an `Anker(karte_id, ort_id)` with geometry
`Punkt | Umriss | Pfad | ohne`. It therefore has a **parent**, a **breadcrumb**, a **distance from
where the party is standing**, and a **render on the tactical canvas**, projected per character
through the same `Sicht` that computes the fog.

**What the user does.** Plays. The threshold is *in the fog*, at a coordinate, as a thin unclosed
ring; it has a distance readout; riding toward it shortens the number; resolving it closes the ring
and mints a paragraph. A player who does not hold a passage naming it sees nothing — not a greyed
ring, **nothing**, byte-identically.

**Where the bodies come from, for free.** `Karte:Andaria` carries **190 markers, of which 187 have no
article, 54 with a paragraph of the author's own prose (9 512 characters)** — RB-12 §5.5, RB-21a
§6.2. A marker is a title *with a position, a faction and a description*. **Those are the best doors
in the corpus and they cost the GM nothing**, and under the champion's model they were invisible,
because the champion's door was a `link_id` in a passage and a map marker is not a link.

**Why only under this thesis.** RB-21a §6.1 killed the containment-defuses-redlinks claim at 14.5 %
and the lineage has had no answer since. This is the answer, and it runs the other way: **containment
cannot order 1 253 doors, but it can order eleven perfectly.** Reachability filters *first*, and what
survives is exactly the set for which containment was always the right tool. RB-21a's *"containment
orders one door in seven"* is not a defeat under this thesis — it is a specification of which seventh
to keep.

**Cost.** ~5 days beyond RB-21a's already-priced `Ort`/`Anker`/`Bezug` tables: the threshold-as-`Ort`
projection, the distance derivation over the `Raum` closure (measured at **0.70 µs/ancestor query**
and **3.18 ms/subtree** at 56 k nodes, RB-21a §5), the ring render, and the L6 anchor-leak rule
(*`Anker` is projected with the `Karte`, never with the `Ort`*). **Risk: medium.**

### 3.3 VF3 · Die Nennung und das Namensregister — absence becomes the densest surface in the product

**This is the feature that answers the attack.** If you delete 678 doors you had better hand something
back, and what you hand back is better than what you took.

**What it is.** A `Nennung` is a first-class object that is **not** an `Entry`: a normalised name, its
display spellings, and every passage that uses it. 689 names, 1 225 citations, no pages, no stubs, no
promises. It is a **concordance** — the index at the back of a real book — and the corpus produces it
for free because it has already been written 1 225 times.

**What the user does.** Hovers or focuses any name anywhere in the product and gets, in the gutter,
**the sentences in her own world that use that word** — projected through her own `Sicht`, so a
player and the GM see different ones. At the table: `/` then three letters, and the eighteen passages
that say `Andarisch` are on screen with **zero page loads** (RB-13 target **M11**: ≤2 interactions to
a fact; this is **1**).

**Why this is the level-up and not a consolation.** Fandom's red link is a 404 — one dead end per
occurrence, 1 991 of them. World Anvil's January-2026 answer is a faster text editor. **Ours is a
concordance**: the same absence, rendered as *density* instead of *debt*. `Gotteserhöhung` is **3
bytes** of article and **8 inbound links** (WELT.md §3.1) — Fandom has no state between "article" and
"nothing" and so it renders as a broken promise. As a `Nennung` it renders as eight sentences from
eight different articles about a central world concept, which is *more* than the 3-byte article ever
said.

**And it is the search spine.** Gate **Search at scale** has been red for five rounds (CHAMPION
§12.4). A name index over normalised strings with passage postings is exactly a retrieval structure,
it is built at import, it is small (**target ≤60 KB raw / ≤20 KB gzipped for the Eron corpus** —
estimated, §12's falsifier is to build it), and it ships inside RB-13 **M6**'s 2 MB whole-corpus
offline budget with room to spare. It does not turn the gate green. It is the first structure in five
rounds that could.

**Why only under this thesis.** The champion had no reason to build it: if every red link is a door
with a ring, a concordance of red links is a second navigation for the same objects. Once 678 of them
are *not* doors, they need a home, and the home turns out to be the best reading surface in the
product.

**Cost.** ~4 days: normaliser (shared with VF1), postings table, the gutter render, the projection.
**Risk: medium** — the count is a denominator and §11.4 says so.

### 3.4 VF4 · Beförderung und Rückstufung — the door set stays small forever

**What it is.** Two one-key, audited, reason-printing gestures over the same table.

- **Beförderung** — the GM (or a player, via a `Vorhaben`) turns a `Nennung` into a `Schwelle`. One
  keypress. It is the escape hatch that makes the classifier's errors cheap, and it is the same
  one-way audited shape as RB-21a's `Ding → Ort` promotion.
- **Rückstufung** — a `Schwelle` nobody has touched in **three sessions** dims and falls back to a
  `Nennung`, automatically, visibly, in the Fällung ritual at the end of the evening. **A door is
  rented, not owned.**

**What the user does.** At 23:41, in die Fällung, she sees three rows fade with a printed reason
(*„seit Sitzung 12 unberührt"*) and can hold any of them open with one key. Nothing is deleted; the
name is still in the register with its 14 citations.

**Why this matters more than it sounds.** The import produces a small set **on day one**. Play
produces new absences forever — a name spoken at the table is a `Nennung` the moment it is minted.
Without a decay rule the door set grows monotonically and this candidate becomes the champion again
in six months. **The scarcity has to be a mechanism, not a starting condition.**

**Why only under this thesis.** A champion whose promise is *every red link is a door* cannot demote
a door: demoting one contradicts the sentence. This candidate's sentence is *most names are not
doors*, so demotion is the thesis running forward rather than an exception to it.

**Cost.** ~2 days. **Risk: low.** The `letzte_beruehrung_session_id` column and one job.

### 3.5 What this level-up is *not*

It is not six half-features. VF1, VF3 and VF4 are three verbs on one table (`Nennung` /
`Referent` / `Schwelle`); VF2 is that table joined to RB-21a's already-ruled `Ort`. **Die
Verjüngungsregel** ([`04`](../../04-die-eine-plattform.md) §6.4) is honoured on the schema and
half-honoured on the surface: one `Vollmacht.anker` variant is **deleted** (§9), the Story zone's red
link is **replaced** rather than added to, and the product gains one genuinely new named surface —
das Namensregister. **Net +1 surface, and §11.3 charges it.**

## 4. Die Stufe — die Leinwand, and this candidate does not refuse it

**The canvas ships. In slice 1. Interactive, in the page.** This thesis does not merely tolerate the
rung — it *needs* it, because a threshold with coordinates that you cannot see is a claim, and this
lineage has enough of those.

### 4.1 What is on screen — der Schwellentisch

An in-page tactical surface, DOM-authoritative per K7, canvas behind the `MapRenderer` boundary
(CHAMPION §9, RB-11 — the boundary is honoured, slice 1's backend is Canvas2D over a vector plate,
Pixi is the slice-2 backend behind the identical interface; gate **K-G8** (`packages/forge` never
imports `packages/chronik`) is untouched).

| Required by the rung | How it appears |
|---|---|
| **A map with a grid** | Square · **hex** (pointy-top, offset coords) · **gridless**, switchable live, no reload. Snap behaviour differs per type; the token's DOM row is unchanged. |
| **Tokens that move** | Drag with pointer **and** move with arrow keys from the initiative list — the keyboard path is the primary one and is what the a11y gate tests. Snap per grid type; ghost preview; drop is a `Zustand` write with the undo ring. |
| **Per-character fog derived from revelations** | **The fog is not geometry.** It is the set of revealed region ids — *which is exactly `Sicht`* ([`04`](../../04-die-eine-plattform.md) §0.6.3, RB-20b: **9.4 ms per incremental reveal**, 386 ms from-scratch union at 800 revelations in a worker). The polygon mask is a derived render artifact. Turning the reader dial re-projects server-side and the canvas redraws from the new id set. |
| **Elevation** | A scalar band on the node (`hoehe_von`/`hoehe_bis`/`ordinal`, RB-21a **R6**), shown as a token badge and a band selector. **The underdark is a sibling of the surface region, not its child** — the band selector demonstrates exactly that, on real data. |
| **An initiative order** | A real `<ol>` with focusable rows, `aria-current`, HP, conditions, and `defeat_pending` requiring explicit GM confirmation (invariant 4). The canvas is the *view*; the list is the truth. |

### 4.2 What is on it that no other candidate this round will have

**Die Schwellen liegen im Nebel.** Eleven thin unclosed rings, at their `Anker` coordinates, each
labelled with its name, its demand (*"14 Artikel nennen es"*) and its distance. A `Nennung` renders
as **nothing** — no ghost, no pin, no dotted circle. **That is the honest render and it is the whole
thesis in one visual decision.**

**Two dials, and the second one is the proof.** A **character dial** re-projects fog *and* thresholds
together, because they are one predicate; a **skin dial** proves K7 (art is content and skin). The
page runs a live in-page **Zwillingsbeweis**: it serialises the canvas's own scene description and
the full DOM for two characters whose held halves are identical but for the passages naming
Blattheim, and asserts byte equality for the non-holder — the same self-checking discipline
round-04's spike-A2 established, now applied to a surface that did not exist last round.

### 4.3 Content, and the licence line that is itself part of the craft

**Real Eron geometry, no Eron pixels.** The plate is drawn from `Karte:Andaria`'s real contract —
`bounds [[0,0],[8192,8192]]`, `origin bottom-left`, `coordinateOrder xy` — and the real marker
positions (`Blattheim [1185.4, 5518.0]`). **The 7.2 MB base JPEG is not embedded, downloaded or
redistributed**, because `media.json` records **0 of 41 files with any licence field** and the wiki
self-declares `Kategorie:Lizenz unbekannt` (WELT.md §3.8, invariant 7). The page prints this, in the
page, as one line under the map:

> *Basisrelief prozedural erzeugt · Originalraster nicht eingebettet (0/41 Dateien mit Lizenzfeld).
> Prosa: Eron Wiki, CC BY-SA 3.0.*

**A product demonstrating its own asset-provenance invariant inside the demo is a stronger artifact
than one that quietly uses the picture.**

### 4.4 The optik level-up, named against specific files

Round 4's artifacts (`round-04/spike-A2`, `spike-B1/B2`) were **document surfaces with live
self-checks** — real contrast measurement across sixteen skin×theme×contrast combinations, a
serialise-and-compare Zwillingsbeweis, a re-executed dice evaluator. That bar is kept and extended,
not replaced. **What is above it, concretely:**

1. **A live spatial surface with real coordinate math** where round 4 had only flow layout — 8192²
   world space, continuous `Vergrößerung` inside one `Karte` with `Anker.ab_zoom` declutter, and a
   **named `Übergang`** (with a URL, a back-stack entry, a breadcrumb entry, honouring
   `prefers-reduced-motion`) between Andaria and a Blattheim floorplan — **never a faked continuous
   zoom**, because OSM, IMDF and Unreal all refuse to fake it (RB-21a §3.5).
2. **A hypsometric plate with real elevation bands** rather than a flat fill, so the band selector
   has something to select.
3. **Typography that survives the real corpus at both ends** — `Gotteserhöhung` (3 B) and `Erismus`
   (56 KB, 67 headings, five levels deep) in the same renderer, with no lead-paragraph assumption
   (M18/Arvex).
4. **Three name states with three distinct non-colour affordances** (weight, baseline, ring), so the
   distinction survives high contrast, greyscale and forced-colours by construction — the failure
   round-04's own overflow-guard break taught (`overflow-wrap: anywhere` on every name node, plus the
   60-character-unbroken-token fixture, carried).
5. **The gutter as a real second column** at ≥1024 px and a disclosure at <1024 px, with `Der
   Streifen` (≤4 % `scrollHeight` growth) still holding.

### 4.5 What the canvas does *not* do in slice 1

No tile pyramid, no KTX2/Basis, no GPU fog texture, no dynamic per-token LOS, no Scene Levels beyond
the scalar band, no 3D, no WFC. **One `Karte`, one level, ≤300 tokens, ≤64 revealed regions.** The
perf harness ships with it and can go red (§12). This is the smallest canvas that is honestly a
canvas, and it is the first one this lineage has drawn.

## 5. How the two halves fuse

**The champion's mechanism is unchanged: five mint gestures table→wiki, three predicates wiki→table,
one citation gesture.** This candidate adds **one object that is simultaneously in both halves** and
therefore fuses them in a way a mint gesture cannot.

> **Die Schwelle ist gleichzeitig eine Lücke in der Enzyklopädie, ein Ring auf der Karte und der
> Anker einer Vollmacht. Ein Objekt, drei Hälften-Rollen, ein Sicht-Prädikat.**

The chain, stated as data flow:

```
Passage nennt einen Namen  →  Nennung (+Vorkommen)
       Nachfrage ≥3, Erreichbarkeit ✓  →  Schwelle  →  Ort  →  Anker  →  Ring im Nebel
                                              ↑                              ↓
                                   Vollmacht.anker                    Sicht projiziert beides
                                              ↓                              ↓
                          Wurf → Prägung (P1) → Passage  → schließt den Ring, nennt neue Namen
```

**The loop closes on itself.** The paragraph minted by resolving a threshold contains new wikilinks;
those become new `Nennung`en; a few clear demand and reachability; a few become thresholds; those get
bodies on the map. **Play does not just write the encyclopedia — it re-draws the map's unresolved
edges.** Nothing in the corpus and nothing in the rival set does that.

1. **21:49** — Sera's player asks who runs the elf realm. Kaya types `/` and three letters:

   Sera holds four of them. Kaya reads one aloud. **Zero page loads, one interaction** (M11).
2. **21:53** — Sera rolls Menschenkenntnis. The card prints `+2 · du hältst 4 Passagen mit
   #demmaros` — `haelt_etikett` over her own projection, unchanged from the champion.
3. **22:10** — the party rides. The Blattheim ring's distance ticks from `3 Tage` to `1 Tag`. Brannt's
   screen shows fog and no ring; he holds no naming passage.
4. **22:31** — they arrive. The ring is inside the fog boundary. Kaya presses the single reveal
   control — **eine einzige Freigabe**: the fog opens *because the knowledge opened*, one control, not
   two (CHAMPION §4.6, unchanged).
5. **22:33** — a roll, a `Ctrl+Enter`, a sealed line released. **Blattheim has a paragraph.** The ring
   closes. The name's state changes from `Schwelle` to `geschrieben` in every one of the fourteen
   articles that referenced it, at once, because they all reference one `Referent`.
6. **22:34 — the fusion beat nobody has shown.** The new paragraph names `Versteck der Flüsterer in
   Blattheim` (a real redlink in the corpus, linked from `Das Kind`). It is a **child place** — its

7. **23:41, die Fällung** — two thresholds untouched since session 12 fade to `Nennung` with a printed

**What the wiki gave the table:** an ordered, distance-ranked, three-item menu that nobody wrote, and
twelve citations in one keystroke. **What the table gave the wiki:** one paragraph, one closed
threshold, one new name, and one door retired. **Both directions in one evening, on real material.**

### 8.5 The universality check (K7 / seat 2)

**Eron is fixture, never subject.** The classifier is a declarative rule package, and the *mechanism*
is `field label → reachability class`, which is language- and genre-independent. The **defaults** are

change:

**If a candidate's answer to universality is "our renderer is generic," it has not been tested. This

```text

                | Passage(pid) | Ort(region_pid) | Keim(titel)
```

**You can no longer anchor an authorisation to a red link. Only to a threshold.** This is the
lineage's own favourite move — *unrepresentability, not policy* (CHAMPION §6). A future round that
wants every red link to be a door again must change a type, in public, with a migration, rather than
relaxing a rule in a code review. `Keim(titel)` survives untouched, so a GM with no wiki at all can
still hand-mint a door, and gate **W0 · die Ankerprobe** still separates *"no wiki"* from *"thesis
refuted."*

Also changed:
- `Link` gains nothing. The link stays anchored on the passage (RB-12 §5.2) — `Nennung` is a
  *projection over* links, not a replacement for them.
- `Passage.gepraegt_durch` gains one value: `schwelle`.
- `Ort.herkunft` gains `{import: wiki, schwelle: referent_id}`.

## 11. Weaknesses

### 11.1 The printed reason is a load-bearing lie waiting to happen — and it is not the hard one

A wrong verdict with a printed reason is worse than a wrong verdict in silence, because the reason
teaches the GM whether to keep reading reasons. `Andarisch · keine Schwelle: nur in Feldern »Sprache«
genannt` is checkable and true. But a GM whose campaign is *about* the extinction of the Andarian
language reads a correct reason attached to a wrong conclusion, and the correctness of the sentence
makes the product feel more confidently wrong. **The reversal key does not fix this**, because the
damage is done in the second she reads it; it fixes the state, not the trust. This is why §7.3 puts
the reason bar (**100/100**) above the verdict bar (**≥0.95**), and it is still only a mitigation.

### 11.2 THE HARD ONE — I shrank the menu and cannot show that appetite grew

**Gate W1 (die Türquote) measures whether ≥50 % of *issued* Vollmachten fire before expiry.** This
candidate does not change issuance, the cap, the key supply, or the player's Tuesday. **It changes
the menu.** The claim that a shorter, ranked, addressed menu produces a higher fire rate is a
folk-psychological intuition — the corpus's own *„Die Tür funktioniert nur, wenn sie selten ist"* is
a GM's judgement, not a measurement, and it is the only evidence this candidate has for its central
behavioural bet.

**If W1 returns the champion's own number, this candidate bought nothing and paid six days for a
classifier plus a permanent obligation to maintain a rule package per world.** The rival's bet
(more keys) fails differently and more visibly — you can *see* whether more keys get used — which
means **the rival's thesis is easier to falsify than mine, and that is a genuine disadvantage of
mine, not a rhetorical one.** The only honest response is to state the falsifier before the fact:

> **If, over four instrumented weeks, the fire rate of Vollmachten anchored to `Schwellen` is not at
> least 15 percentage points above the champion's `RoterLink`-anchored baseline, die Schwelle is a
> tidier version of the same product and should be graded as one.**

I cannot make that number less scary and I am not going to try.

### 11.3 Die Verjüngungsregel is only half-honoured, and the accretion is real

Three new tables, one new declarative package, one new named surface (das Namensregister), one new
canvas. Deleted: exactly one enum variant. **Net +1 surface and +3 tables**, in a lineage whose own
strategy document says round 4 added 8 oracle rows and 5 tables and that this must stop
([`04`](../../04-die-eine-plattform.md) §8.3). The defence — that `Nennung`/`Referent`/`Schwelle` are
one table read three ways — is true and is also exactly what every accreting round says.

### 11.4 The concordance count is a denominator wearing a coat

*„18 Artikel nennen Andarisch"* is a count. `Kein Nenner` (CHAMPION §6.5) makes denominators
unrepresentable in a player payload via `NurLeitung<T>`, precisely so a player cannot infer the size
of what she does not hold. The answer here — compute the count over the reader's own projection — is
correct and **opens a second channel in the other direction**: a player who saw `4` on Saturday and
sees `5` on Tuesday has learned that something arrived, without being told what. That is a
low-bandwidth channel and it is a real one, it is the same class of defect the champion's own
`tuer_zustand` oracle was flagged for, and **`nennung_zaehler` must be an `oracles.yaml` row with a
green test or CI fails.** Not closed.

### 11.5 The canvas is in slice 1 and its number is invented

Fourteen days for the first canvas anyone in this lineage has drawn, against a ~120-day tactical
estimate that has been priced and never built for four rounds. **The estimate is a guess in an
uncalibrated unit** and RB-18 says exactly what that is worth (*"a schedule denominated in an
uncalibrated unit is not a bill; it is a hope with columns"*). RB-20b's own honest line —
*"I rendered zero Pixi frames today"* — still stands for the whole crew. **S-K1 (one day, one frame)
is the falsifier and it must run before this document's slice is planned against.**

### 11.6 A demoted name has no address, and some tables will read that as the product deciding

The champion let a table walk at anything red. This one refuses for 661 names. A player who says
*"I want to find out about Andarisch"* gets a concordance and no die roll. There is a real kind of
table — the improvisational, follow-the-players'-curiosity kind — for which that refusal is the
product telling them what their story is about. `Beförderung` is one keypress, but it is the **GM's**
keypress, which means this candidate quietly moves a small amount of narrative authority back toward
the GM at the exact moment the lineage spent a round moving it toward players.

### 11.7 Carried, unimproved, and named so it cannot be missed

`Sicht` unbuilt and unmeasured for a **fifth** round (S-P1 unrun; 04 §8.5 makes it a gate on round 6)
· the mint rate never measured · Search at scale red for a fifth round (this candidate builds a
structure that could help; it does not run the gate) · the 02:00 long-form writer still unserved ·
right-to-erasure vs. a durable `Wurf` · the revocation race, still unattacked · the art pipeline
unpriced except for maps (€2,700–8,200 for ~90 assets, **€0 until a paying user exists**) · K2 still
called the go-to-market and still unpriced · no market evidence for any of this.

## 13. Creative extensions

### 13.1 Der Index — a campaign's own back-of-book index, printed

The concordance is already the structure a real book's index is. **Print it.** 689 names, each with
its citation numbers into die Chronik's own footnote numbering (which already exists, and whose CI
already asserts *footnote count == chip count*). A GM ends a campaign with a bound volume that has an
**index** — the one artifact of a real encyclopedia nobody in this market can produce, because
producing it requires an addressable atom below the page and everyone else's atom is the page.

### 13.2 Die Schwellenmappe — a tradeable unit smaller than an evening

Die Ausgabe (CHAMPION §10.9) sells a week. **Die Schwellenmappe sells eleven doors**: names, reasons,
demand counts, anchors, parents — **and no prose**. It is a *map of what is missing* in someone's
world, which is the cheapest thing to author and the most useful thing to receive, and it is
structurally non-importable by every rival because none of them models an absence as an object.
Declarative, closed-schema, ten hostile fixtures, same `Geschlossene Tüte` discipline.

### 13.3 Die Fremdnennung — a contentless popularity signal over absences

When two tables import the same source wiki, the name index can show **which names other tables
promoted to thresholds** — a count, nothing else. **No passage crosses, no prose crosses, no identity
crosses**; the only thing shared is the structural fact that somebody, somewhere, decided a name was
worth walking at. It is the best prep signal in the product and it costs zero new privacy surface,
because absences have no contents to leak. (And it is the one place where being default-unindexable —
RB-13 §7's permanent loss — costs us nothing, because the signal never needed a reader.)
