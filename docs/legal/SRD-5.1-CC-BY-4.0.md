**System Reference Document 5.1 (SRD 5.1)** by Wizards of the Coast LLC.

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 ("SRD 5.1") by Wizards

at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

Atlas Chronicles changes the source material by expressing character-sheet and resolution concepts
as bounded declarative fields, collections, formulas, actions and presentation metadata; labels are
arranged for the Atlas user interface. The application identifies the package as **5E-compatible**
and does not imply endorsement by Wizards of the Coast.

The package intentionally does not include material from Dungeons & Dragons books that is outside
SRD 5.1. Campaign settings, non-SRD subclasses, non-SRD feats, non-SRD monsters, non-SRD spells and
other closed material must be supplied separately by users only where they have the right to do so.
