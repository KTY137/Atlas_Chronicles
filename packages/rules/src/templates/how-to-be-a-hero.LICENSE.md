Changes: Für Atlas Chronicles in deklarative Regeln und Bogenfelder übertragen;
Erläuterungen neu formuliert; bestätigte Regelversion festgeschrieben. At effective skill
70, 97 is a critical failure under the approved inclusive boundary. The later unapproved
revision begins at 98; this template does not silently adopt it. Explicit adaptation
precedence: aptitude checks never critically succeed, and aptitude zero fails even on
natural 1. Critical damage is `2 × (Nd10 + agreed bonus)`; doubling the bonus is an explicit
adaptation interpretation. A single loss above 60 HP causes unconsciousness; the table
confirms application of that status. Catalogue and characters are original examples.
